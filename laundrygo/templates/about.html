Our story

A laundry counter that comes to you.

LaundryGo is an MCA mini project for APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University. It models a real Palakkad laundry that replaced walk-in queues with scheduled pickups, status tracking and a small operations desk.

The platform is a complete web application: customers book washing, ironing and dry-cleaning; riders follow a daily schedule; admins manage orders, payments and service rates — all from the browser.

How we run

People, bags, a hub — and software that keeps them in sync.

Every order gets a unique ID, a pickup window and a delivery window. Status moves from Pending to Delivered so both the customer and the shop floor see the same truth. Payments can be collected as cash on delivery or marked paid online.

Submitted by Muhammed Ajzel as a demonstration of web development, database design and a practical local problem.
